Figures come from SAP Monitor Process Orders (COOISPI), order types YB05 (Bulk) and YB06 (Bag), grouped by basic start date. Quantity is the delivered quantity in tonnes. The latest day or two is refreshed from SAP every 30 minutes; earlier days come from the stored year history. The page reloads itself every 15 minutes. The same data is in SAP_PP_Dispatch_YTD.xlsx.
